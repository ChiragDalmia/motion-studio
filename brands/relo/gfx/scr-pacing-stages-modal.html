Pacing Stages

Pacing shows whether you're on track to be ready by your target exam date. It moves through three stages as your mastery grows:

Launch

Get early momentum, level up your first concepts to Intermediate, building to 5 to complete Launch.

Study You're here

Pace every concept toward two targets: Intermediate+ first, then Proficient, before exam day.

Final Review

Prove you're ready by passing full practice exams before test day.
