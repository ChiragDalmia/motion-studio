An associate broker in Alberta is charged with a criminal offence that is unrelated to their real estate business. According to the Real Estate Act Rules, what is this individual's notification requirement to RECA?
No notification is required because the charge is not related to their real estate activities.
They are only required to notify the Executive Director if they are convicted.
They must immediately notify the Executive Director of the charge.
They must notify the Executive Director within 30 days of the charge.
Proceed
Explanation
Notes
Topic Targeted

(Correct) They must immediately notify the Executive Director of the charge.

Section 40(1)(a) of the Real Estate Act Rules states that an industry member must immediately give the Executive Director written notice after being charged with an offence under the Criminal Code (Canada) or other similar legislation.

(Incorrect) They are only required to notify the Executive Director if they are convicted.

The Real Estate Act Rules require notification upon being charged, not upon conviction. This ensures RECA is aware of any situation that may affect an industry member's suitability to trade in real estate as soon as it arises.

(Incorrect) They must notify the Executive Director within 30 days of the charge.

The timeline required by the Real Estate Act Rules is "immediately," not within 30 days.

(Incorrect) No notification is required because the charge is not related to their real estate activities.

The requirement to notify RECA applies regardless of whether the criminal charge is related to the individual's real estate business, as any criminal charge pertains to their character and suitability for licensing.
