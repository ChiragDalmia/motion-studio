Factor 1: Degree of Annexation

The first factor is the Degree of Annexation. This is a factual assessment of how the item is attached.

How stable is it?

Is the attachment firm and secure, or loose and temporary?

How easily can it be removed?

Does removal require specialized tools or significant effort?

Would removal cause significant damage?

Would it leave a large hole in the property or destroy the item itself?
